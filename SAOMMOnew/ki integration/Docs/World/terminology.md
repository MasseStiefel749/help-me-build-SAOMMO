# World Terminology

**Status:** Active  
**Authority:** Band 4 §13, Band 7  
**Date:** 2026-08-15

Consistent terms for documentation, dialogue, and AI generation. Prefer these over synonyms.

---

## Core Terms

| Term (EN) | Term (DE) | Definition |
| --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Fallen sword civilization | Gefallene Klingenkultur | Ancient society that collapsed due to combat obsession; see lore anchor |
| Reconstruction | Wiederaufbau | Modern era's effort to rebuild civil life after the fall |
| Artifact | Artefakt | Powerful relic from the ancient civilization; hidden, guarded, narratively significant |
| Blade culture | Klingenkultur | Social respect for sword mastery — distinct from blind worship |
| Adventurer | Abenteurer | Player-facing role; seeks skill, reputation, Artifacts |
| Starting Reach | Brunnfeld-Gürtel | Provisional name for first test region |

---

## Place Types

| Term | Definition |
| ---- | ---------- |
| Safe zone | Combat-free or trivial-threat hub (e.g. Brunnfeld) |
| Training grounds | Controlled space for weapon practice (e.g. Klingenhof) |
| Wilderness border | First outdoor combat tier (e.g. Grauwaldrand) |
| Ruin / dungeon | Instanced or gated challenge space with higher threat |
| Region | Connected play space with internal progression |
| Major region | Future large-scale zone — not used for Starting Reach |

## Terms to Avoid in SAOMMO Tone

Per Band 7 §9 — do not use in official world text:

* Grimdark excess, torture for shock
* Ironic meta-humor breaking immersion
* "Chosen one" framing at game start
* Generic MMO filler ("kill 10 rats" without world reason)
